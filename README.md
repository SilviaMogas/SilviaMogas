## 🧭 About me

**Founder · Web3 Builder · Product Operator · Hackathon Winner**

I build products and prototypes at the intersection of **Web3, digital identity, trust infrastructure, consumer adoption, and real-world use cases**.

My background is both strategic and product-driven. I come from almost 20 years across **marketing, partnerships, product, growth, events, and venture building**. That gives me a practical lens when I build: I care about the user, the market, the narrative, and the system behind the product.

In Web3, I focus on one core question:

> **How do we make trust programmable, usable, and understandable?**

I use hackathons and technical sprints to test ideas fast, validate use cases, and move from concept to working product.
